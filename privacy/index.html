Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sadapanna ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with this website and our apps and games (collectively, the "Services"). We build our Services with privacy in mind and aim to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

Website. This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand traffic and improve the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to work without cookies, but it may process standard technical and usage information such as page views, referrers, browser details, and IP address information. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may also collect standard technical information (such as IP addresses) in server logs. Please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Apps and games. Each of our apps and games may have its own privacy policy describing the specific data it handles, which will be linked from its store listing. In general, our apps are designed to store your data on your device wherever possible and to collect only what is needed for the app to function.

2. How We Use Information

Where information is collected, we use it only to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Services;
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs;
  • Respond to your messages when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps may rely on trusted third-party services (for example, app stores, crash reporting, or advertising providers). Where a specific app uses such services, its own privacy policy will identify them and link to their policies. This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for basic traffic measurement, but it does not embed advertising or other marketing trackers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above. We take reasonable measures to protect any information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at hello@sadapanna.com.
