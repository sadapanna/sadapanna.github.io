Arrow Flux — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sadapanna ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with Arrow Flux (the "App"), a mobile puzzle game. It supplements our general Privacy Policy, which covers our website and Services as a whole; where the two differ, this policy governs the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require an account to play Arrow Flux, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other contact information through the App.

Information stored on your device. Arrow Flux saves your scores, progress, and settings locally on your device using standard device storage. This information is not sent to us and stays on your device unless you uninstall the App.

Advertising identifiers. Arrow Flux displays ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS, where available), IP address, and other technical or usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Section 3 below.

2. How We Use Information

Where information is collected, it is used only to:

  • Save your game progress and preferences on your device;
  • Display and measure advertising within the App;
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Advertising and Consent

Arrow Flux uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may work with third-party ad networks that collect and process data (including advertising identifiers) to serve ads that may be personalized based on your activity.

Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area and the UK), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before requesting personalized ads. You can review or change your ad-consent choices at any time from the App's Settings screen ("Privacy Options" or similar), or reset your device's advertising identifier and ad personalization settings in your device's system settings.

For more information about how Google and its partners collect and use data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

Arrow Flux requests network access so it can load ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photos.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Flux is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

6. Data Retention and Security

Game progress and settings remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We take reasonable measures to protect any information handled in connection with the App, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw ad consent at any time as described in Section 3. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at hello@sadapanna.com.
