Questions, answered

The things creators ask us before they try one.

Are these tools really free?

Yes. No account, no sign-up, no trial, no paid tier, no feature locked behind one. Exports carry a small Sadapanna watermark, and that's the entire arrangement.

Do I need to install anything?

No. All three run in a browser tab on desktop. They use modern browser video encoding, so a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox works best.

Does my text or data get uploaded?

No. Everything is laid out and rendered on your own machine, and projects save into your browser's own storage. Nothing leaves your device — there's no server to send it to.

Can I make YouTube Shorts with them?

ChartFlow and ReadFlow both export at 9:16 for Shorts, as well as 16:9 and 1:1. InkFlow exports at whatever size you set, vertical included.

Can I use the exports in a monetised video?

Yes. Anything you make is yours to use commercially. We'd love a link if you feel like sending one, but nothing requires it.

Something missing?

If there's a shot you keep making the hard way, tell us. Half of what's on this page started as somebody's complaint in an email.
