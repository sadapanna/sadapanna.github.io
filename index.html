Exploring

Our whole rule: only build things we'd genuinely use ourselves.

The name — Sadapanna, sometimes written Sada Panna — started as a side project that refused to stay one. Today it's two products; where it goes from here is deliberately open — apps, games, services, whatever clears the bar.

The bar itself doesn't move: no ads wedged into every corner, no selling your data, no features nobody asked for.

Questions, answered

The stuff people ask us most — including how to spell the name.

What is Sadapanna?

Sadapanna is an independent studio building apps, games, and other digital products. We're starting with Arrow Flux, a game about timing, and Trail Mark, a simple habit app — with more planned.

Is it spelled "Sadapanna" or "Sada Panna"?

Both refer to the same studio. The official name is written as one word — Sadapanna — but people often search for it as two words, Sada Panna. Either way, you're in the right place.

What does Sadapanna build?

Right now, two products: Arrow Flux (a timing-and-aim game, in closed testing) and Trail Mark (a habit tracker, in development). Beyond those, we're exploring more apps, games, tools, and services.

How can I contact Sadapanna?

Email is the best way to reach us at hello@sadapanna.com. We read everything, even if a reply takes a day or two.

When do Sadapanna's products launch?

We build slowly and on purpose. Arrow Flux is in closed testing and Trail Mark is in active development — follow along or drop us a line and we'll let you know when they're ready.
