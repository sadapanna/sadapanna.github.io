Questions, answered

The stuff people ask us most — including how to spell the name.

What is Sadapanna?

Sadapanna is a small independent studio. We make Arrow Flux, a timing game on Google Play, InkFlow, a free text-animation tool, GeoFlow, a free geometry canvas, ReadFlow, a free maker for article-highlight videos, ChartFlow, a free maker for animated chart videos, and Trail Mark, a habit app that's still in development. We also write a blog called Mind.

Is it spelled "Sadapanna" or "Sada Panna"?

Both refer to the same studio. The official name is written as one word — Sadapanna — but people often search for it as two words, Sada Panna. Either way, you're in the right place.

What does Sadapanna build?

Right now: Arrow Flux, a timing game that's live on Google Play; InkFlow, a free browser tool that turns text into handwriting animations; GeoFlow, a free geometry canvas where constructions move when you drag them; ReadFlow, a free browser tool for making article-highlight videos for YouTube; ChartFlow, a free browser tool for animated charts and bar chart races; and Trail Mark, a habit tracker we're still building. More will come, but we don't announce things before they're real.

How can I contact Sadapanna?

Email is the best way to reach us at hello@sadapanna.com. We read everything, even if a reply takes a day or two.

When do Sadapanna's products launch?

Arrow Flux is already out on Google Play, and InkFlow, GeoFlow, ReadFlow and ChartFlow are free to use right here on the site. Trail Mark is still in development and we don't have a date for it yet. Drop us a line and we'll tell you where things stand.
