Apps and games are chapter one. Tools, services, experiments — the list is long, and we're not in a rush to shorten it.

Exploring

Our whole rule: only build things we'd genuinely use ourselves.

Sadapanna started as a side project that refused to stay one. Today it's two products; where it goes from here is deliberately open — apps, games, services, whatever clears the bar.

The bar itself doesn't move: no ads wedged into every corner, no selling your data, no features nobody asked for.
