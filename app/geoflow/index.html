How it works
  • Point / Line / Circle / Shape — tap to build. Hovering shows what you'd snap to: midpoints, thirds, intersections, "on circle"…
  • Move — drag any blue point; everything built from it follows. Double-click a point to rename it, or a graph to edit its formula.
  • Select things → actions appear. In Move mode every click adds to the selection (click again to remove; click empty space to clear). E.g. a segment → Midpoint, ⊥ bisector, ÷n; a point + a line → ⊥ / ∥ through it; 3 points → Triangle, Angle, Circle through 3; two curves → Intersect.
  • Drag on empty space (in Move mode) box-selects everything inside. To pan in Move mode, hold Shift while dragging — every other tool pans on a plain drag, and pinch/scroll works everywhere.
  • Shift-drag from a point — rubber-band a size, release, then pick Circle / △ / ◻ / ⬠ / ⬡ / n from the menu.
  • Angle snapping — while drawing a line it clicks to 0°/45°/90° and to ⊥ / ∥ of lines on screen; the tag names the line (e.g. "⊥ to TU") and that line glows. Finishing on such a snap creates a linked ⊥/∥ — it stays true when dragged. Select it → 🔗 Unlink to break the link (works on any derived object). Dragged points snap to grid crossings; circle radii snap to half-units.
  • Graph — type f(x) like x^2/4 or sin(x). Points snap onto the curve; intersect it with lines or other graphs.
  • 📏 — toggle live sizes (lengths, radii) in grid units.
  • Shape tool presets — pick △ ◻ ⬠ ⬡ (or type n) in the row under the toolbar, then tap one corner and the opposite corner, like drawing a box: the shape fits inside. Drag any corner to rotate/resize (angles click to 15° steps); drag the ✛ center handle to move the whole shape — and turn it into a real point from its menu if you need it.
  • Dragging snaps too — a moving point grabs onto other points, midpoints, intersections, curves and the grid, with the same named tags as when placing. Select several points and drag any of them to move them all together. Hold Ctrl/Cmd at any time — dragging or placing — for completely free movement, no snapping. Dragging a line’s endpoint also snaps the line itself to 0°/15°/30°/45°/90° and to ⊥/∥ of other lines.
  • Angles & tangents — select two lines → Angle between. Put a point on a circle or graph, select it → Tangent here; the tangent works with "Angle between" like any other line. A point on a line (or curve) also offers ∠ N° line — type an angle and get a line held at exactly that angle, with a live angle marker.
  • Unlink — selecting anything built from something shows Unlink; it names every link in plain words ("D is the midpoint of a") and you choose which to break, or break all.
  • Keyboard — tools: M/V move, P point, L line, C circle, S shape, G graph (or 1–6). Arrows nudge the selection (Shift = bigger steps) · U unlink · Del delete · Esc cancels a step · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · + / − / 0 zoom.
  • Work saves automatically — ☰ opens your files. Everything runs offline.
