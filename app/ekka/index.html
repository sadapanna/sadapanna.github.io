How to play

Goal: be the first to get rid of all your cards.

  • On your turn, play a card that matches the top card's colour or its number / symbol.
  • Nothing to play? Tap the deck to draw one. If it fits, you may play it straight away.
  • Hop skips the next player. Flip reverses the direction. +2 makes the next player draw two and miss a turn.
  • Wild lets you pick the colour. Wild +4 does that and hands the next player four cards.
  • Down to one card? Hit Ekka! If someone taps Catch before you do, you draw two.

Playing together: one person creates a room and shares the code (or the QR). Everyone joins on their own phone. It works best when you are on the same wifi, and each hand stays private on its own screen.

No accounts, no ads. Your devices talk to each other directly; a matchmaking server only introduces them by room code.
